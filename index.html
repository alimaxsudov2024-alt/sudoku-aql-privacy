Privacy Policy - Sudoku Aql

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Sudoku Aql ("the app") is a Sudoku game for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses.

1. Information we collect

The app does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or any other personal information. Game data such as your scores, stars, best times, settings, selected language and a saved unfinished game is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server we control, and it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Advertising

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, prevent fraud and (where allowed) personalize ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and information about how you interact with ads. We do not receive this information. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Some ads are optional videos that you choose to watch in exchange for an extra hint or life.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with third parties, other than the advertising service described above.

4. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
